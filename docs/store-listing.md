## Short description (80 characters)

Never lose track of a prayer again. Gentle check-ins, honest qaza ledger.

## Full description (draft)

When a prayer window ends, Salah Tracker asks: did you pray? One tap
answers. A miss becomes a dated qaza you can settle later — nothing is
lost, nothing is punished.

- Prayer times calculated on your device, offline, from your location.
  No account, no server, no ads, no analytics.
- A clear Today screen: the current prayer, the next prayer, and anything
  still waiting for your answer.
- A Recovery ledger: your oldest qaza first, settle one at a time, with
  Undo on everything.
- A Journey calendar: streaks and a day-by-day record you can correct.
  Add back-dated qaza for a whole date range.
- Dark mode, and reminders you can turn off any time.

All data stays on your device. Backup is a file you export yourself.
